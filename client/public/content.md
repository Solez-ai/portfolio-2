# SOLEZ — Samin Yeasar, Full Stack Developer & Founder

SOLEZ is the portfolio of **Samin Yeasar**, a Bangladesh-based full-stack developer, solo founder, and open-source maker. Samin builds helpful technology across frontend systems, backend services, developer tools, data interfaces, programming language infrastructure, and native desktop interaction. He is the **Co-Founder and CTO of MentorMind**, a platform intended to introduce a new culture of help to Bangladesh, and a **Co-Founder of Team Fälschen**, a two-person research team that explores robotics, physics, and AI.

## Technical Capabilities

Samin works across React, Next.js, TypeScript, JavaScript, HTML, CSS, Tailwind CSS, Node.js, Python, Rust, Supabase, Firebase, PostgreSQL, Docker, AWS, GCP, Vercel, Git, C++, WebAssembly, and VS Code Extension APIs. The work is guided by practical product outcomes, durable engineering decisions, and the belief that technology should make useful help easier to find and give.
