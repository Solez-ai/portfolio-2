About SOLEZ

Samin Yeasar, known as SOLEZ, is a Bangladesh-based full-stack developer, solo founder, and open-source maker. His work treats software as both a technical material and a practical public service: the goal is to build tools that are legible, durable, and genuinely useful. The portfolio documents work across frontend engineering, backend systems, data interfaces, developer tooling, programming-language infrastructure, and native desktop interaction.

Samin is the Co-Founder and CTO of MentorMind, where he leads technology, product, and engineering for a platform intended to introduce a new culture of help to Bangladesh. He is also a Co-Founder of Team Fälschen, a two-person research team that connects robotics, physics, AI, human signals, and the physical world. This mix of product work and technical research informs a practice that can move between interface details, systems design, and the human outcomes behind a build.

Core technical capabilities include React, Next.js, TypeScript, JavaScript, Node.js, Python, Rust, PostgreSQL, Supabase, Firebase, Docker, AWS, GCP, WebAssembly, C++, Git, and VS Code Extension APIs. Selected projects include Nodex, Vell, URLPad, PySketch, and 3 Drag Win. For current code and public activity, visit GitHub; for collaboration, use the contact page.
