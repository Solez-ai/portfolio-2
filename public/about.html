About Samin Yeasar (SOLEZ)

My name is Samin Yeasar, and I go by SOLEZ (also styled as Solez-ai, SOLEZ-AI, or solez.ai). I am a Full Stack Developer and Co-Founder based in Bangladesh. I build low-latency web applications and developer tools using React, TypeScript, Rust, Node.js, and cloud platforms like AWS, GCP, and Vercel.

What I Do

I am the Co-Founder and CTO of MentorMind, a platform introducing a new culture of structured mentorship and peer help to Bangladesh. I lead the technical architecture, product engineering, and full-stack delivery at MentorMind.

Prior to founding MentorMind, I worked as a Junior Web Developer at Solven.ai for six months in 2025, where I shipped client-facing features and built internal automation tooling.
